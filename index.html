Tracker Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Soluntion Labs ("we," "us," or "our") built Tracker Radar to help you find Bluetooth trackers that may be following you. This policy covers Tracker Radar for Android and for iPhone (both with the identifier com.soluntion.trackerradar) and explains what information each version handles and how. The two versions work differently in a few places, mainly because Android and iOS give apps different access to Bluetooth and location, and because only the Android version shows ads. Where they differ, the section below says so.

On both platforms, Tracker Radar has no backend of its own and no account. Everything it detects, stores, and analyzes about the Bluetooth devices around you stays on your device and is never sent to us.

INFORMATION THE APP READS

Tracker Radar scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices to detect trackers such as AirTags, SmartTags, Tile devices, Google Find Hub trackers, and other BLE beacons. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to us, to advertisers, or to any other third party.

On Android, the app reads the device names, MAC addresses, and signal strength broadcast by nearby BLE devices.

On iPhone, iOS does not give apps the MAC address of nearby devices. The app reads the data nearby devices broadcast, their signal strength, any name they broadcast, and a random identifier that iOS assigns to each nearby device for this app only. To identify a tracker that broadcasts nothing recognizable, or when you tap "Play Sound," the app briefly connects to that device over Bluetooth to read which services it offers or to ask it to play its sound. The app asks for Bluetooth permission the first time it scans.

INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

If you label a device, mark a tracker as safe or ignored, or enable background monitoring, that information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. On iPhone, the app also stores its scan report (trackers it flagged and when) and how many trackers of each kind you've told it you carry. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of it. On iPhone, like other app data, it is included in your device's own backups (iCloud or your computer) if you use them; those backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

The scan report leaves your device only if you choose to share it, using your device's standard share feature. We do not receive a copy, and what happens next is controlled by the app you shared with.

LOCATION PERMISSION

On Android, the operating system requires apps to hold a location permission in order to perform Bluetooth Low Energy scans on many device OS versions. Tracker Radar requests this permission solely to satisfy that OS requirement. The Android app does not access, collect, or store your GPS location, and does not use the location permission for any location-tracking purpose.

On iPhone, Bluetooth scanning does not need location, and the app works without it. If you turn on background checking and allow location access, the app uses it for one purpose: to notice that you have moved, so it can check again for trackers while it is closed and judge whether a tracker has been moving with you. It uses only iOS's coarse location services for this: significant location changes, visits, and a small zone around where you are, which iOS watches and wakes the app when you leave. The app does not track your route, never stores your location or coordinates, and never sends them anywhere. You can change or remove location access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.

BACKGROUND MONITORING & NOTIFICATIONS

On Android, if you enable background monitoring (a Premium feature), Tracker Radar continues scanning for BLE trackers while the app isn't open, using a persistent notification as required by Android for foreground services.

On iPhone, iOS does not allow continuous scanning while an app is closed. If you turn on background checking, the app checks for some kinds of trackers when iOS wakes it, for example after you move (see Location Permission above). This is free on iPhone.

On both platforms, if the same tracker appears to be moving with you for an extended period, the app sends a local notification, after asking for your permission to send notifications. This monitoring and its notifications happen entirely on your device; no notification passes through our servers or anyone else's.

SUBSCRIPTIONS & PAYMENTS

Premium subscriptions are purchased and processed through the store you buy them from: Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple's App Store on iPhone. Tracker Radar never receives or stores your payment card details — all payment information is handled directly by Google or Apple, subject to their own privacy policies. The app only asks the store whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Premium features and restore your purchase if you reinstall the app or use another device signed in to the same Google account or Apple Account.

ADVERTISING (ANDROID FREE VERSION ONLY)

The iPhone app shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The free version of Tracker Radar for Android shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), and information about how ads perform and how the app is used, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Ads are never shown to Premium subscribers, and the ad service isn't started for them.

Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Android app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options when that option appears. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Your Bluetooth scan results, device names, and labels are never shared with advertisers.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We don't require an account or registration. We don't use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs on either platform. On Android, the only third-party SDKs that handle data are Google Play Billing (payments) and Google AdMob (ads, free version only). On iPhone, the app contains no third-party SDKs; purchases go through Apple's own StoreKit. We don't sell your personal information, and everything Tracker Radar detects, stores, and analyzes about the Bluetooth devices around you stays on your device.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Tracker Radar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as the updated version appears on this page.
