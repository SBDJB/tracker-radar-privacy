Tracker Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Soluntion Labs ("we," "us," or "our") built Tracker Radar as a Bluetooth tracker detection app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Tracker Radar scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices to detect trackers such as AirTags, SmartTags, Tile devices, and other BLE beacons. During a scan, the app reads device names, MAC addresses, and signal strength broadcast by nearby BLE devices. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third-party server — Tracker Radar has no backend and no analytics.

If you label a device, ignore a tracker, or enable background monitoring, that information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

LOCATION PERMISSION

Android requires apps to hold a location permission in order to perform Bluetooth Low Energy scans on many device OS versions. Tracker Radar requests this permission solely to satisfy that OS requirement. The app does not access, collect, or store your GPS location, and does not use the location permission for any location-tracking purpose.

BACKGROUND MONITORING & NOTIFICATIONS

If you enable background monitoring (a Premium feature), Tracker Radar continues scanning for BLE trackers while the app isn't open, using a persistent notification as required by Android for foreground services. If the same tracker is detected near you continuously for an extended period, the app sends a local notification. This monitoring and its notifications happen entirely on-device.

SUBSCRIPTIONS & PAYMENTS

Premium subscriptions are purchased and processed through Google Play Billing. Tracker Radar never receives or stores your payment card details — all payment information is handled directly by Google Play, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We don't require an account or registration. We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We don't sell or share your data, because we don't collect any data from you in the first place — everything Tracker Radar detects, stores, and analyzes stays on your device.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Tracker Radar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as the updated version appears on this page.

CONTACT US

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@soluntion.com.
